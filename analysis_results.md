## 3. Inventory Policy Simulation & Scenario Analysis

We simulated three alternative inventory management strategies over a test window using the digital twin model:
1. **Scenario 1 (Current Policy):** Reorders 400 units from the supplier once local inventory drops below 150.
2. **Scenario 2 (Transfer Policy):** Allows physical inventory transfer between warehouses before ordering from the supplier if one warehouse has excess stock.
3. **Scenario 3 (Increased Reorder Quantity):** Reorders 600 units (instead of 400) to keep higher safety stock buffer.

## 4. Policy Recommendation

> [!NOTE]
> **RECOMMENDED STRATEGY: Scenario 2 (Inventory Transfer Policy)**
>
> **Rationale:**
> * **Maximizes Profit:** Delivered a net profit of **\$332,189.58**, outperforming the current policy by **\$3,434.54** and the increased reorder strategy by **\$5,474.66**.
> * **Maximizes Customer Service:** The service level increased to **99.23%** (vs. 96.46% current), while reducing stockouts from **798 units to just 174 units** (a **78% reduction** in lost demand).
> * **Efficiency:** Did not require inflating the average inventory levels or overloading warehouse capacity (utilization remained stable at 27.01%).
